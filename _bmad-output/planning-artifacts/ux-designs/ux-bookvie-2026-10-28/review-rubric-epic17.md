## Overall verdict

The update is close to usable for Stories 17.1 and 17.2. Every new token reference resolves, both contrast rows recompute to the stated values, the new strings pass the copy rules, and committing DESIGN.md keeps the tokens guard green, because only `components` changed. One high finding needs a decision before 17.1: the spine says every Stats change replaces the history entry and that focus stays on the bar, but the shipped `PageHeading` moves focus to the `h1` on every location change, a replace included. Seven medium findings sit where the ATDD needs an exact string or behavior: the Custom heading for one month or one day, the row link's exact name, which bar Tab lands on, the focus ring on a selected bar, empty columns now exposed to assistive tech, the hover-only guard on the row, and a Flow 16 number that can't be true.

## 1. Flow coverage — adequate

Checked: the new source (the stats plan) maps to Flow 16, and Flow 7 gains one line. Flow 16 has a protagonist and setting, a precondition, seven numbered steps, a climax (step 6) and a failure path (a half Custom link). The history steps follow Stats → Address: the range and bar changes replace the entry, the Book is pushed, Back returns to the replaced Stats entry, **Show the whole range** replaces again, and one more Back leaves Stats. Step 7's focus and announcement match Stats → Show the whole range and Announcements. Steps 2, 3 and 7 agree on the numbers: 9 books, 3,120 pages, three in March, *Refactoring* in February and April.

### Findings
- **medium** Step 4 calls March "the tallest bar, explained by *Designing Data-Intensive Applications*' 611 pages", but the numbers rule it out. The range has 3,120 pages and March has 912, so February and April share 2,208, and at least one of them has 1,104 pages or more, which is taller than March (EXPERIENCE.md → Flow 16, steps 2 and 4). An ATDD fixture built from the flow would contradict its own story. *Fix:* drop "the tallest bar", or change the totals so March is tallest (for example 9 books, 2,310 pages: March 912, February and April under 912 each, 1,398 between them).

## 2. Token completeness — adequate

Checked: every `{...}` reference in both files resolves against DESIGN.md's frontmatter (scripted with pyyaml; 240 references). The only unresolved one, `{components.favourite-star.on}`, was already unresolved before this change (Epic 16) and isn't an Epic 17 finding. All 23 `stats-finished-row` keys and the 4 new `monthly-bar` keys are referenced in the prose. No color, typography, rounded or spacing primitive was added. `designTokens()` in `e2e/library.ts` reads only those four sections, so `app-shell.spec.ts` AC-1 stays green when this DESIGN.md is committed before any story. The new `components` keys are additive and don't affect any `designComponentValue` or `designComponentColor` call in the e2e suite. Contrast was recomputed with WCAG relative luminance and sRGB alpha blending: `accent` at 0.35 over `accent-soft` is `#B1C0B2` (light) and `#506553` (dark). Dimmed vs track: 1.56 / 1.90. Selected vs dimmed: 2.87 / 2.79. Both match the table. The row stars match the shipped Book detail stars exactly: 14px, 2px gap, filled `accent`, outline `input-border`, and Highlight / CanvasText under forced colors.

### Findings
- **medium** The focus ring can't sit "outside the selected ring" with the tokens given. `{components.highlight-ring.ring}` is a box-shadow that covers 0–4px outside the track (2px `page`, then 2px `accent`). `{components.focus-ring}` is a 2px outline at a 2px offset, so it covers 2–4px: exactly the ring's `accent` band. A focused, selected bar then looks the same as a selected bar. Under forced colors it gets worse: the box-shadow is dropped and the selection becomes "a 2px `Highlight` outline", and the focus ring is also an outline on the same track. One element has only one outline, so either focus or selection disappears (DESIGN.md → Stats finished list → Bar focus; Colors → Forced colors → Stats). The mock shows focus only on an unselected bar (April), so it doesn't settle this. *Fix:* give the bar's focus ring its own offset when the bar is selected (for example `outline-offset: 6px`, which leaves a 2px gap outside the ring), and add it as a key (`monthly-bar.selected-focus-offset`). Under forced colors, draw the selection on a different box from the focus, for example the selection as a `Highlight` outline on the track and the focus as the system focus outline on the column button. With 24 bars at 1024px the columns are about 32px wide with an 8px gap, so say that the ring may overlap the gap.

## 3. Component coverage — adequate

Checked: Monthly bars and the finished list each have a DESIGN.md Components row with a subsection, and an EXPERIENCE.md Component Patterns row with the Stats subsection. Selected, dimmed, focus, dark theme, forced colors and no motion are covered. The row's date ("14 Mar 2026" = `shortDate`), its stars (`role="img"`, "4 stars" / "1 star" = `starsLabel`) and the author rule ("and 1 other" / "and N others", from `spineName`) match the shipped code. The heading strings, "Show the whole range", the announcements and the bar names have no exclamation mark, greeting or praise, so they pass `copy.test.mjs`'s text rules. The chart's keys (`ArrowLeft`, `ArrowRight`, `Home`, `End`) aren't single-character shortcuts.

### Findings
- **medium** "Hover underlines the title only" runs into `copy.test.mjs`'s hover-only rule. Every `:hover` selector must have a `:focus` twin in the same selector list, so `.row:hover .title` alone fails `npm run check`. The mock's `li a.row:hover .t` would fail the same way. The spine is silent on whether focus underlines the title too, so the builder has to guess, and an ATDD that asserts "no underline on focus" would be red forever (DESIGN.md → Stats finished list → Link states). *Fix:* "Hover and keyboard focus underline the title; focus also draws `{components.focus-ring}` around the whole row", the same as the Main book card title ("underline on hover and focus only").
- **medium** The row link's accessible name is described ("its text and the stars' name, in that order") but not given as a string. The ATDD needs an exact name, and how Chromium joins the parts (spaces, the " · ") depends on markup the spine doesn't fix (EXPERIENCE.md → Stats → The row). *Fix:* give one exact name, for example "*Dune* Frank Herbert 14 Mar 2026 · 611 pages 4 stars", say whether it comes from the content or from an `aria-label`, and give one for an unrated row and one with no author.
- **medium** The chart stops being `aria-hidden`, but the spine only says what the bar buttons expose. A bar with no finishes "is drawn as before, but isn't a button", so its "0" and "Jul" (and the year line on the first bar and on each January) become loose text inside the "Finished by month" group, read out between the buttons. That repeats the hidden table, which stays the overview (plan D16) (EXPERIENCE.md → Stats → Choosing a bar). *Fix:* "Inside the group, only the bar buttons are exposed. An empty column, and the visible labels inside a button, are `aria-hidden`; the hidden table covers empty periods."
- **low** Component names differ: EXPERIENCE.md's row is "Finished list", DESIGN.md's is "Stats finished list", while Component Patterns says "Visual specs: DESIGN.md → Components, same names" (EXPERIENCE.md → Component Patterns, Finished list row). *Fix:* name it "Stats finished list" in both.
- **low** No author: the row's line 2 is "the author", but `firstAuthor` can be empty (`spineName` handles `''`). *Fix:* "With no author, line 2 is left out" or "keeps its height, empty", to match the unrated rule.
- **low** "**Show the whole range** … right after the heading" doesn't say it is a sibling of the `h2`. If it sits inside, the heading's name becomes "Finished in March 2026 Show the whole range" and an exact heading test fails (EXPERIENCE.md → Stats → Show the whole range; DESIGN.md → Stats finished list → Heading). *Fix:* "a sibling after the `h2`, never inside it".
- **low** The memlog's default "no hover style on a bar beyond the pointer cursor" isn't in either spine (`.memlog.md`, assumptions entry). *Fix:* add it to DESIGN.md → Stats finished list → Selected bar.
- **low** Bar names don't say how large numbers are written. The visible value uses `formatCount` ("1,204"), so the name should too. *Fix:* add "1,204 pages, with en-GB grouping as in the visible value" to the naming example.

## 4. State coverage — thin

Checked: Stats now has rows for an empty range, All time with nothing finished, a selected bar and a bad address. The failure state ("Couldn't load" with Try again) is unchanged and needs nothing new, since `shown` is null on failure and the list renders from `shown`. Range-change clearing (D15), bad-address fallbacks (D14), the announcement silence on load (all four entries) and the Address rules are stated.

### Findings
- **high** The Address rule breaks the shipped route-change focus. `PageHeading` focuses the `h1` whenever `useLocation().key` changes after any navigation (`components/PageHeading.tsx`, `lib/page.ts`). React Router gives a replace a new key too. `main.tsx` also calls `markNavigated()` on the first location change of any kind, a replace included. So once Stats writes the range or the bar to the address, every preset press, date edit and bar press moves focus to the `h1`. That contradicts "focus stays on the bar" (Pressing), "focus moves to the bar that was selected" (Show the whole range) and Flow 16 steps 3 and 7. A bad or plain address fixed by a replace on first load would also focus the `h1` on that first load (EXPERIENCE.md → Stats → Address, Pressing, Show the whole range; Accessibility Floor → Structure, "On a route change, focus moves to the new page's `h1`"). *Fix:* state that a Stats address replace is not a route change: it doesn't move focus, doesn't count as the first navigation, and doesn't scroll. Name `PageHeading.tsx` and `main.tsx`'s `markNavigated` as existing leaves that change in Story 17.1 (plan section 5's list), so the ATDD covers it: press This month, and focus stays on This month.
- **medium** The Custom heading's format is given only for two dates in different months ("1 February – 15 April 2026") and across a year end. It isn't given for a range inside one month (1–15 March 2026) or a single day (From = To), which Custom allows (EXPERIENCE.md → Stats → Heading). The ATDD needs the exact strings. *Fix:* add both, for example "Finished 1 – 15 March 2026" (or "1 March – 15 March 2026") and "Finished 15 March 2026", and say the year is written once when both dates share it.
- **medium** Which bar Tab lands on is ambiguous when the two rules disagree. "Tab lands on the selected bar" and "After that, Tab returns to the last focused bar" conflict when March is selected and the user arrows to May without pressing it, then Tabs out and back. It is also unclear what happens when the last focused bar is gone after a range change (EXPERIENCE.md → Stats → Keyboard). *Fix:* order the rules, for example: "The chart's Tab stop is the last focused bar while it still exists; otherwise the selected bar; otherwise the latest bar with finishes. A range change resets it."
- **low** A plain `/stats` "opens on This year with no bar", but the spine doesn't say whether the address is then replaced with `?range=thisYear`. That rule is stated only for a bad address. The Stats nav link while already on Stats is also unstated: a `Link` to `/stats` pushes, so Back would return to the old range, against "Back from Stats leaves Stats in one step" (EXPERIENCE.md → Stats → Address). *Fix:* say whether `/stats` stays as typed or is replaced, and whether the nav link on Stats resets to This year with a replace.
- **low** Back from a Book "returns to the same range, bar and list", but the spine doesn't say where the page is scrolled. With 40 rows the list is long, and focus goes to the `h1` (Flow 16 step 6, "exactly as Oleg left it"). *Fix:* say that the page opens at the top with focus on the `h1`, as after any route change, or that scroll is restored.
