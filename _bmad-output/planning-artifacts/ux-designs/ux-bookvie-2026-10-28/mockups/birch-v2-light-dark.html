Where a read spine gets its colour
From the cover (default when the book has a cover image). The dominant cover hue is softened toward the birch palette. The Hitchhiker's Guide to the GalaxyOne Hundred Years of SolitudeNorwegian Wood and most others.
Calm curated palette (when there's no cover):The Mythical Man-MonthThe Pragmatic ProgrammerThinking in Systems: A PrimerDeep WorkA Philosophy of Software DesignThe Elements of Style
Customised by you (always wins):CosmosThe Hobbit
Curated palette: sage #749076 slate #5B7590 clay #9B6753 ochre #B48A45 plum #7A6283 teal #4B7C7B moss #6C7650 stone #6F7375
Spine sizingEvery spine is 28px wide. Height is set by the title length: 11px vertical text plus padding, rounded up to an 8px step and kept within 120–200px, so neighbouring books stay in proportion. A title too long for 200px wraps onto a second line inside the spine (for example Gödel, Escher, Bach: An Eternal Golden Braid) instead of making the spine taller.
Label contrastThe label is white or ink, whichever contrasts more with the spine colour. If neither reaches 4.5:1, the colour is nudged slightly darker or lighter until one does. Custom colours follow the same rule.
At ~500 booksAbout 35 spines fit on a plank at 1280px, so 500 books fill roughly 15 shelves. The bookcase card then shows three shelves and scrolls inside itself. Shelf rows render lazily, and the meter stays one bar.
Spine popoverOpens on click, Enter or Space, anchored above the spine with a pointer. It shows details and one action that fits the book's state: unread → Set as Up next; Up next → Start reading; waiting → Resume; Main book or finished → no primary. Closes on Esc or an outside click, and focus returns to the spine.
Dark themeEvening, Dark selected. Shows the brief finish moment on a spine instead of the popover.
