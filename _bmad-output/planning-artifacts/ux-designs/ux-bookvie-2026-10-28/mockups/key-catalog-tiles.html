Covers keep their own shape and sit on one bottom line per row; the 2:3 box never changes size, so a loading cover (Station Eleven, flat fill with the hairline) moves nothing. Every tile reserves the 11px reading-bar slot, so captions line up across each row. Finished books carry the ribbon in their spine color, with a pale light spine-label-strip halo outside its edge line so it shows even on a cover of the same color (The Road, Piranesi); The Left Hand of Darkness (Main book, 64%) and Gödel, Escher, Bach (paused, page 88 of 777) carry the reading bar; Dune is a paused re-read and carries both. Unread books have no mark. The Mythical Man-Month has no cover and is finished: typeset cover with the paper plate. The Elements of Typographic Style has no cover and is unread: outlined typeset cover, no plate. Long titles and authors end in an ellipsis on one line.
Each tile is one link; its name is the spine's name, for example Persuasion by Jane Austen, finished, The Left Hand of Darkness by Ursula K. Le Guin, Main book, 64%, Dune by Frank Herbert, finished, waiting for you, page 120; a re-read that is the Main book reads …, finished, re-reading, 12%. Accelerate by Nicole Forsgren and 2 others, not read yet keeps the co-authors the caption drops. The ribbon and the bar are aria-hidden; the cover image has empty alt. The list is named Tiles, 109 books. Columns: 5 at 1024px, 7 at 1280px, 8 at 1440px.
