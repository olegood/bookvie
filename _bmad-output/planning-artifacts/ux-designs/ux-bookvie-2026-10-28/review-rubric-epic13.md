That covers EXPERIENCE.md Foundation and IA edits, Voice rows, Component Patterns → Auto-backup line, the State row, Flows 13 and 14 and Agent conversations, plus DESIGN.md `components.auto-backup-line`, the Backup page and Auto-backup line rows, and the Do/Don't row. ## Overall verdict

The update is usable by the stories. Every new token reference resolves, the line's colors reuse contrast pairs that are already computed, the words-only, no-red and no-warning rules hold in every new string, and the component name is identical in both files. There are no critical or high findings. The medium findings are in two places that Story 13.5's and 13.8's P0 tests will hit: the state rules (Failing vs Stalled once an error is older than 26 h, the 26 h boundary written two ways, unreadable signals), and the restore outcome table (no `couldnt_save` or Dropbox-failure row, and a `restore_not_confirmed` hint the skill can't reliably choose). The Flow 14 pick-list example also contradicts the flow it illustrates.

### Findings
- **medium** The pick-list example doesn't fit Flow 14, which it illustrates. Flow 14 step 1 has Oleg choose "the Nov 19 Dropbox copy", but the example list (Agent conversations → Restore, step 1) has no Nov 19 entry. The list says "newest first" but puts "Nov 21 10:04 · safety copy" third, after Nov 21 and Nov 20. The safety copy line also has no size, although the memlog says each entry has date, source and size. A skill story or an eval written from the example would encode all four slips (EXPERIENCE.md → Agent conversations → Restore, step 1; Flow 14 steps 1 and 5). *Fix:* use a list that Flow 14 can show, for example "1. Nov 21 · Dropbox · 73 MB / 2. Nov 21 · this computer · 73 MB / 3. Nov 20 · Dropbox · 73 MB / 4. Nov 19 · Dropbox · 72 MB / 5. Nov 18 10:31 · safety copy (before a restore) · this computer · 72 MB", in strict date-and-time order, every entry with a size.
- **low** Flow 13's failure path blames sleep: "the Mac stayed asleep through 09:00 and nobody reported anything for two days → Stalled". D6 and the guide's troubleshooting ("The Mac was asleep at 09:00 → launchd runs it on wake; nothing to do") say sleep doesn't cause Stalled. The guide gives Stalled's causes as a run that crashed before it could report: `claude` missing from launchd's `PATH`, a tool missing from `--allowedTools`, or Claude Code signed out (EXPERIENCE.md → Flow 13, failure path). *Fix:* "after a Claude Code reinstall moved `claude`, the 09:00 runs crashed before they could report → "Auto-backup: Stalled · no backup since Nov 19"; the guide's Stalled entry names the `PATH` fix."

## 4. State coverage — thin

Checked: Off on a dev server (listed first, which is right: D8 and FR-29 "never from development", and it wins even when the job is installed), Off when the job isn't installed or the computer isn't a Mac (D10), Failing, Waiting, On after an upload or a skip, and Stalled. Also the copy plurals, a manual Export (it doesn't move the line, and an upload doesn't move "Last export", per FR-29 and D9), a restore (it moves neither, per FR-29), and "Couldn't load". The order Off → Failing → Waiting → On → Stalled matches D11's table.

### Findings
- **medium** The spine doesn't say what happens to an error older than 26 hours. The order puts Failing before Stalled, so an error reported days ago stays Failing for good. The Stalled row's "When" copies FR-29 word for word ("No upload, skip or error in 26 hours"), which says an error older than 26 h is Stalled. D11 says the opposite ("no success or skip in 26 h, **no error reported**"). If Failing wins, the line keeps a stale reason ("it tries again tomorrow at 09:00") after the job itself has stopped reporting. Story 13.5's P0 "Badge states" test has to pick one reading (EXPERIENCE.md → Auto-backup line, state table, Failing and Stalled rows). *Fix:* commit to one rule and word both rows to match. Recommended: "Failing: the last report is an error less than 26 hours old. Stalled: no report of any kind in 26 hours (an older error included)", with Stalled's detail adding the last error's reason if there was one. Or keep Failing indefinitely and reword Stalled's When to "No upload or skip in 26 hours, and the last report wasn't an error". Either way, record the choice as a deviation from FR-29's or D11's wording.
- **medium** The 26 h boundary is written two ways. The On row says "less than 26 hours ago", so exactly 26 h is Stalled. The When bullet says "Exactly 26 hours since the last good run is still On; one minute later it's Stalled", which implies minute resolution and leaves 26 h 00 min 30 s undefined. The plan's test ("exactly 26 h on either side of the boundary") needs one exact rule (EXPERIENCE.md → Auto-backup line, On row and **When.** bullet). *Fix:* On row: "An upload or a skip 26 hours ago or less". Bullet: "The boundary is inclusive and exact: at 26 h 0 min 0 s it's On, at any later instant it's Stalled; computed on the server against its clock (plan 13.5)."
- **medium** The spine doesn't say what the line shows when a signal can't be read. The launchctl probe could fail or time out. The upload fields in `local-state.json` could be missing or malformed (AD-15 reads a malformed file as "no export", but this case has no rule). The last-run time could be in the future after a clock change. Each case lands silently in some state, and a working backup must not read as Off, nor a broken one as On (EXPERIENCE.md → Auto-backup line; State Patterns). *Fix:* add a **Signals.** bullet. For example: "A probe that errors counts as not installed (Off · not set up on this computer); unreadable upload state counts as never ran; a last-run time in the future counts as now ("today at …", Foundation → Dates). Nothing says it failed."
- **low** Waiting never times out. A job that has never run successfully, for example because `claude` was missing from `PATH` from day one, shows "first backup at 09:00" forever, long after 09:00 has passed. D11 and the guide ("Waiting that doesn't turn On after 09:00") accept this, but the spine doesn't say so (EXPERIENCE.md → Auto-backup line, Waiting row). *Fix:* add "Waiting doesn't turn Stalled: bookvie doesn't know when the job was installed; the guide's Waiting entry covers a first run that never comes."
- **low** "it tries again tomorrow at 09:00" puts a relative day into a reason that is set when the run reports and then shown for as long as Failing lasts. Viewed the next morning before 09:00, or after a manual run before 09:00, "tomorrow" is wrong (EXPERIENCE.md → Failing reasons, "Upload didn't match…" row). *Fix:* "…Nothing in Dropbox was deleted; the next 09:00 run tries again."
- **low** Two reason rows break the list's own rules. The Failing reasons bullet says "an upload that failed always says nothing was deleted", but the sign-in row ("Dropbox sign-in expired. Run bookvie-dropbox auth in Terminal.") doesn't say it, although `upload_backup` failed. "Export all data still works." is stated unconditionally, but if the cause was storage that can't be read (the Flow 7 failure), the page's Export fails too (EXPERIENCE.md → Failing reasons table). *Fix:* narrow the rule to "an upload that started and failed", or add "Nothing in Dropbox was deleted." to the sign-in row. Change the export row to "bookvie couldn't write the backup file. Try Export all data on this page."

## 7. Inheritance discipline — adequate

Checked: `sources` adds the plan, and it resolves. FR-29 and FR-30 are cited by number. ### Findings
- **medium** The restore outcome table is missing three outcomes the tools can return. (a) `couldnt_save`: AD-14 makes a write fault before the commit a 503 `couldnt_save`, and `restore_backup` applies through the same handler (D13), so the skill needs a sentence for it. (b) Dropbox failures during `list_backups` or `download_backup`: an expired sign-in, no network, or "no backups found" in either source. (c) No backups at all. Without these rows the skill will improvise copy for the most likely real-world failures (EXPERIENCE.md → Agent conversations → Restore, step 4 table). *Fix:* add rows: "Couldn't save | "Couldn't restore. Your data wasn't changed. The safety copy is at *path*."", "Dropbox unreachable or sign-in expired | the pick list shows local backups only, with "Dropbox backups aren't available: *reason*. Run bookvie-dropbox auth in Terminal." when it's the sign-in", and "No backups | "No backups to restore yet.""
- **medium** The `restore_not_confirmed` row has the skill add "Run it in the Claude Code terminal, not VS Code or claude -p." only "for a client that can't ask". D15 folds decline, cancel, a client without elicitation and an auto-decline in print mode into the one code, and an auto-decline looks exactly like a user's decline. The skill therefore can't tell when to add the hint. A story that ships the row as written will either never show the hint or show it after a real decline (EXPERIENCE.md → Agent conversations → Restore, outcome table, second row). *Fix:* have `restore_backup`'s result carry a reason (`declined`, `cancelled`, `unsupported` when the client didn't advertise elicitation) and add the hint only for `unsupported`. Note that a print-mode auto-decline reads as `declined` and gets no hint, and point to the guide's restore troubleshooting for it. Flag this for Story 13.6, which checks the SDK's behavior.
- **low** `restore_unsafe` isn't named. The table maps "Not a bookvie file or damaged" without codes, while D14 lists three refusal codes. AD-14 says `restore_unsafe` shows `restore_invalid`'s copy, so the copy is right, but the mapping is implicit (EXPERIENCE.md → Agent conversations → Restore, outcome table). *Fix:* add the codes in brackets: "Not a bookvie file, damaged, or unsafe (`restore_invalid`, `restore_unsafe`)" and "Newer version (`restore_newer_version`)".
- **low** The file-name rule contradicts the examples. The section's intro says "its file name appears only in the pick list and the safety copy's path". The pick-list example shows no file names. The preview's first line ("Restore bookvie-backup-2026-11-19.zip (Dropbox, Nov 19)?") and the auto-backup report ("Uploaded bookvie-backup-2026-11-21.zip … deleted bookvie-backup-2026-11-18.zip") do (EXPERIENCE.md → Agent conversations, intro; Restore step 2; Auto-backup report). *Fix:* say where file names do appear (the preview's first line, the safety copy's path, the run report), or take them out of the preview and the report.
- **low** Naming a backup by date alone is ambiguous. A safety copy and a daily backup can share a date (Nov 21 and "Nov 21 10:04"), and every daily backup exists both on this computer and in Dropbox. "Replace all data with the backup from Nov 21?" then doesn't say which one. "Added since Nov 19" also reads as excluding Nov 19, while Books added on Nov 19 after 09:02 are lost too (EXPERIENCE.md → Agent conversations → Restore, steps 2–4). *Fix:* name a safety copy with its time and kind in the preview, confirmation and outcome ("the safety copy from Nov 21 10:04"). Write the loss as "added since this backup (Nov 19, 09:02)". Say how a local and a Dropbox copy of the same day are listed (two entries, or one "this computer and Dropbox" entry). Cap the list (local daily backups are never pruned), for example "the 10 newest, then "and N older"".
- **low** The absolute date and time formats are new and not in Foundation → Dates, which defines only relative times. The new forms are "Nov 19", "on Nov 19", "Nov 21 10:04", 24-hour "09:02" and "today at 09:02". There is no rule for the year (a Stalled line or a backup more than a year old, or one across the Dec/Jan turn, shows "Nov 19" with no year, while Foundation says "over a year ago" past 365 days), and nothing says whether the month name follows the browser's locale (EXPERIENCE.md → Foundation → Dates; Auto-backup line **When.**; Agent conversations intro). *Fix:* add to Foundation → Dates: "Absolute dates are "MMM D" in English ("Nov 19"), with the year ("Nov 19, 2025") when it isn't the current year; times are 24-hour "HH:MM"; date and time are joined by a space ("Nov 21 10:04"), or by "at" after "today"/"yesterday"."
- **low** The spine supersedes D11's colored dots explicitly (DESIGN.md), but not D11's wording ("Auto-backup isn't set up", "Last upload today 09:02 · 3 copies in .backups", "No backup since Nov 19"), and it calls the component "line" where the plan and FR-29 say "badge". The plan's P1 docs test checks "every badge state has a troubleshooting entry with its exact wording", so someone writing the guide from the plan would copy D11's strings. The memlog also says On after a skip "keeps the last upload's date", while the spine shows only the check time and keeps the count (EXPERIENCE.md → Auto-backup line, intro). *Fix:* add to the subsection's intro: "The plan's and PRD's "badge" is this line; its wording here supersedes D11's, and the guide (`docs/dropbox-backup.md`) quotes these strings." Reconcile the memlog's skip note with the On row.
