Epic 17: the finished list and the selected bar

Options for the two visual choices that plan step 2 leaves open (stats-finished-books-plan D3, D7, D10). Only existing tokens are used. Stars are always sage accent, never gold. A finished Book is always read, so its typeset cover is always the read variant: its spine color with the paper plate. At thumbnail size the plate holds no text. Pick one row and one bar style, or mix them.

1. The list row

Each row: the cover or typeset cover, the title, the author, the pages, the finish date and the rating. The whole row is one link. The third Book is unrated, so it shows no stars.

R1: compact, one line (32×48 thumbnail)

The title and author on one line, with the date, pages and stars after them. It's dense: a long year fits in about two screens.

R2: two lines, like the Up next row (44×64 thumbnail, the existing thumbnail size)

The title over the author, with the date and pages over the stars on the right. It reuses the Home section pane's thumbnail and row rhythm. It's roomier and reads more like a memory than a ledger.

R3: ledger, date first (32×48 thumbnail)

Fixed columns: the date, the cover, the title and author, the pages right-aligned, then the stars. It scans best by date, but looks like a table without being one.

2. The selected bar

March is selected. The other bars with finishes are dimmed, and July has no finishes. Every option also marks the selection with more than color (DESIGN.md Do's: "a border or bar as well as a fill"). The last block shows option B2's keyboard focus on April.

B1: others dimmed, plus a sage underline under the selected period

This is the segmented control's selected treatment (a 2px accent bar along the bottom) moved onto the period label. It's the quietest.

B2: others dimmed, plus the highlight ring around the selected bar

This reuses {components.highlight-ring}, the page gap and then the 2px accent ring, which the app already uses for "this one".

B3: whole columns dimmed (labels too), plus a selected column like a chip

The selected column gets the chip's selected fill and border. It's the strongest, but dimming the labels lowers their contrast below 4.5:1, so the dimmed values would need the hidden table to carry them.

Focus (shown with B2): the focus ring on April while March stays selected
