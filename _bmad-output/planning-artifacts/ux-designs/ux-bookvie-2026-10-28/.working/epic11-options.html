1. Favicon drawing (D6)

All three use the sage accent, with accent-dark under a dark color scheme, and the wordmark mark's 2px corner (scaled). The light parts are holes, so the tab strip shows through them. Each option shows 16px (actual tab size), 32px (retina) and 64px on a checkerboard.

F1

Tile with a spine cut-out

The wordmark's sage square, grown to fill the icon, with one spine and its plank cut out of it. The closest to the existing mark, and the solidest silhouette at 16px.

F2

One spine on a plank

A single sage spine with a rounded top, standing on a plank. Its label strip is cut out. Literal, but narrow at 16px.

F3

Three spines on a plank

Three spines of different heights on one plank: a small shelf. Reads as "books", and the mixed heights echo the bookcase. Busier at 16px.

In a tab strip. The first tab is production; the second is dev, drawn in amber with the DEV · title from D8.

2. Dev amber (D7)

The dev favicon and the DEV pill use the same amber. Each value is computed against bookvie's surfaces and Chrome's tab strip. Text under 4.5:1 doesn't pass for 11px pill text. An icon only needs 3:1.

A

#9A6A12 · dark #E3B45C

vs surface-inset 4.49 (just under 4.5, so it can't be the text color of an outlined pill) · white on it 4.73 · vs light tab strip 3.60 · dark twin vs dark strip 8.39

B

#A8701A · dark #D9A54A

The brightest, and the most "amber". vs surface-inset 3.99 · white on it 4.20 (both fail as text) · vs light tab strip 3.21 · dark twin vs dark strip 7.23. It could only be used where no text sits on it or in it.

C

#8C5E0E · dark #EBC27A

The deepest, close to bronze. vs surface-inset 5.36 · white on it 5.64 · on the tinted pill fill 4.77 · vs light tab strip 4.30 · dark twin vs dark strip 9.61. Passes every pill variant below.
