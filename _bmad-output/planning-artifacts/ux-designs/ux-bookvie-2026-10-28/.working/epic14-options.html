Epic 14 — card note boxes: the open visual choices

Built from the approved mockups (canvas "Card notes boxes — mockups"). Each option shows a matched box ("3 of 12", lifted) and two boxes without a match, in light and dark. Tab through to see the focus ring on a faded box. Contrast numbers are computed for the faded title and count line on the label strip.

1 · The fade (D11)

Opacity on the whole box also fades the label. The mockup's 0.38 leaves the title at 2.18:1 in light. In every option the fade goes on the box's parts, never on the link, so the focus ring stays full strength.

2 · The cards in the dark theme

The box front keeps the Book's exact color and the label strip stays paper in both themes, like a spine. The cards peeking out are open.

3 · An edge on the box front

A user can pick any color, including near-white or a pale yellow. Spines carry a fixed spine-edge line for that reason. The mockup's box has none.
