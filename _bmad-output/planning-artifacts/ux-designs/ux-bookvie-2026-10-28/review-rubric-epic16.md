## Overall verdict

The update is close to usable for Stories 16.1 and 16.2. Every token reference resolves, every number in the Favourite gold table recomputes to the stated value, and D1–D11 all land with the same names in both files. Three high findings need a decision before the stories start: the optimistic star breaks architecture AD-7 ("No optimistic updates"), which plan step 4 doesn't list for change; committing the two new `colors` keys turns the token comparison in `app-shell.spec.ts` red until Story 16.1 ships `tokens.css`; and the cover star is placed on the cover box, while the ribbon and EXPERIENCE.md place things on the visible cover. Seven medium findings sit where the ATDD needs an exact string or behavior: announcement timing, focus after an unstar with the chip on, the couldn't-save line when the popover has closed, twin names with ", favourite", exact name matching, the existing rating stars, and the legend.

## 2. Token completeness — adequate

Both new colors have light and dark hexes. Recomputed with WCAG relative luminance: `favourite` on `surface` 4.41 / 6.82, on `page` 4.02 / 7.62, on `accent-soft` 3.63 / 5.27, on `surface-inset` 4.19 / 7.24; `ink-secondary` outline on `surface` 6.13 / 7.61; `#9A7120` on paper `#FBF8F1` 4.16; `#5F635C` on paper 5.78. All match the table. The spine mark fits: on the shortest marked spine with the longest label (152px tall, strip ending 116px from the top) the mark's top is 22px above the plank, so it never touches the strip. The typeset plate starts 34px down, and the star with its halo ends at about 31.5px, so it clears the plate.

### Findings
- **high** `e2e/library.ts` `designTokens()` reads every `colors` key from DESIGN.md at test time, and `app-shell.spec.ts` AC-1 expects each as a custom property in `tokens.css`. `tokens.css` has no `--color-favourite` today. As soon as this DESIGN.md is committed (docs go straight to master), `npm run check` and CI fail until Story 16.1 lands. The Dev amber section avoided exactly this by keeping its colors as component literals "until a story ships it" (DESIGN.md → `colors.favourite`, Colors → Favourite gold). *Fix:* either add the two lines to `tokens.css` in the same commit as the spine, or keep `favourite` and `favourite-dark` out of `colors` for now (a comment, or literals in `components.favourite-star`) and let Story 16.1 move them into `colors` and `tokens.css` together. Say which in the Favourite gold paragraph.
- **low** The contrast table puts the List row on `surface`, but the List table has no background and sits on `page` (`CatalogList.module.css`). The gold star on `page` is in the table, but the unstarred outline on `page` (5.58 / 8.50) isn't (DESIGN.md → Colors → Favourite gold). *Fix:* move "List row" to the `page` row and add "Outline (`ink-secondary`) on `page` | 5.58 | 8.50 | 3.0".
- **low** Three tokens are never referenced in prose: `on`, `spine-mark-background` and `spine-mark-star-color`. The prose writes `#9A7120` and `spine-label-strip` by value instead (DESIGN.md → Spine → Favourite mark). *Fix:* use `{components.favourite-star.spine-mark-star-color}` and `{components.favourite-star.spine-mark-background}` in the Favourite mark bullet.
- **low** Target sizes don't agree. The tile button's target is "32px around the star", a literal with no token, while `button-size` is 34px. The List row uses "the same plain button", so a 34px target in a row that is about 36px tall with `vertical-align: baseline` can make starred rows taller (DESIGN.md → Favourite star, tile and List bullets). *Fix:* add `tile-target: 32px`, and say the List button's target is at least 24px and never grows the row.
- **low** `tile-halo` is a literal `#FBF8F1`, while the ribbon's halo references `{colors.spine-label-strip}`. "1.5px stroke" also doesn't say how it is drawn for the outline-only star, which has no fill to hide the inner half the way the ribbon's halo does (DESIGN.md → `components.favourite-star.tile-halo`). *Fix:* write `'1.5px {colors.spine-label-strip} outside the star's edge, glow 0 0 1.5px {colors.spine-label-strip}'`, and say an outline star gets a paper stroke under its grey stroke, wider by 2 × 1.5px.

## 3. Component coverage — adequate

Checked: Favourite star has a DESIGN.md Components row and subsection and an EXPERIENCE.md row and subsection, with the same name. The Favourites chip, the spine's Favourite mark, the Spine popover, Book detail, Catalog tile and List view are updated in both files. Forced colors, dark theme and no motion are covered. The popover change fits `SpinePopover.tsx`: the blur handler closes on any Tab out, so "Shift+Tab from the star closes" falls out of the DOM order.

### Findings
- **high** DESIGN.md places the cover star "`tile-inset` in from the cover box's top and left edges". The box is 2:3, and the cover image is bottom-centered inside it (`.frame`, `object-position: 50% 100%`). A wider cover, such as Manning's (about 4:5, 49 Books), leaves an empty band at the top of the box, so the star would float above the cover on the page, while the ribbon "sits on the visible cover, not on the empty part of the box". EXPERIENCE.md says "on the cover, at the top left, opposite the finished ribbon". The star is also a sibling of the link, so it can't sit inside `.frame`, which is in the link (DESIGN.md → Favourite star, Catalog tile bullet; EXPERIENCE.md → Favourite star → Where). *Fix:* decide one: (a) on the visible cover, inset from its top-left corner like the ribbon from its top-right, and say how the sibling gets the cover's rectangle; or (b) on the box, and say that on a wide cover the star sits above the visible cover. Make EXPERIENCE.md say the same.
- **medium** The bookcase legend gains "Favourite" in EXPERIENCE.md, but DESIGN.md's Bookcase row still lists the legend as "finished · not read yet · Main book, filled to its % · up next · waiting for you", with no star swatch. Neither file says whether the item shows when nothing is starred (EXPERIENCE.md → Bookcase → Markers; DESIGN.md → Components → Bookcase). *Fix:* add "· favourite, with a mini paper square and star" to the DESIGN.md legend list, and say it is always shown, like the other items.
- **medium** Book detail already has stars: each Reading's rating ("Finished 14 Aug 2019 · ★★★★★"), drawn as `accent`-filled stars in the Finish dialog. A gold favourite star now sits in the same page's title row. Neither spine tells them apart, and "stars" in Book detail → Content means ratings (EXPERIENCE.md → Book detail; DESIGN.md → Finish dialog, Readings). *Fix:* one sentence in Favourite star: "Not a rating: rating stars stay sage in the Readings and the Finish dialog; only the favourite star is gold." Add "rating stars" to the Don't column next to "gold text".
- **low** The forced-colors bullet covers the halo and spine square, but not the chip's always-gold star or the plain-button stars on Book detail, the popover and List (DESIGN.md → Forced colors). *Fix:* "Every favourite star, the chip's included, is `CanvasText`."
- **low** "It is a group of its own" (EXPERIENCE.md) and "no group name of its own" (DESIGN.md) could be read as a `role="group"` with no name. Today every chip group has `role="group"` and `aria-labelledby` (`FilterChips.tsx`) (EXPERIENCE.md → Filter chips). *Fix:* "A lone toggle button, not in a `role="group"`; it is its own group only for the AND rule."

## 4. State coverage — thin

Checked: starred and unstarred on every surface, hover and focus-within reveal, no-op save, failure with rollback, the chip with nothing starred, the chip with a search and other chips, Clear filters, reload, Home's slice and the meter. Missing exact behavior is listed below.

### Findings
- **medium** The announcement's timing isn't stated. The star changes on press, but does `status` say "*Dune* is a favourite." on press, or after the save and refetch? AD-7 says "A success live-region announcement is made only after the refetched views have rendered", and Live regions says both regions are emptied before every save attempt. If it is said on press and the save fails, the user hears "is a favourite" and then "Couldn't save" (EXPERIENCE.md → Favourite star → Click; Accessibility Floor → Live regions). *Fix:* "The star changes on press; `status` speaks only after the save succeeds and the views have rendered. On failure only the `alert` speaks." Say what two quick presses announce: only the last value's sentence.
- **medium** With the chip on, focus goes to "the nearest remaining Book in sort order", which doesn't say what wins when both neighbours remain. Focus after close still says "Spine popover → Any close → Its spine" and "Anything whose opener no longer exists → the page `h1`", which contradicts the new rule for a popover unstar (EXPERIENCE.md → Favourite star → With the Favourites chip on; Accessibility Floor → Focus after close). *Fix:* "the next Book in sort order, else the previous one, else Search titles", and add a Focus after close row: "Spine popover | Unstarred with Favourites on | The next remaining spine, else the previous, else Search titles".
- **medium** The couldn't-save line has no place when the popover has already closed: after a popover unstar with the chip on (the popover closes first), or after Esc or a click away before the save returns. The State row also says "Focus stays on the star", while the chip-on bullet moves focus away (EXPERIENCE.md → State Patterns → Couldn't save; Favourite star → With the Favourites chip on). *Fix:* "If the popover is closed when the save fails, the line shows under the filter row, as on Tiles and List. Focus stays where it is." Define "goes at the next action" as "is emptied at the next save attempt or filter change".
- **medium** The twin rule doesn't say whether ", favourite" counts. Today twins are "same title, author part and status" (`lib/catalog.ts`). If favourite counts, starring one of two twins removes the other's "edition 1" qualifier, so an unrelated Book's name changes. "After any twin qualifiers" also reads oddly, since the status already follows them (EXPERIENCE.md → Component Patterns → Spine; Tiles → Name). *Fix:* "Twins are found on the name without ", favourite"; the favourite ending comes last, after the status: "Dune by Frank Herbert, edition 1, 1965, finished, favourite"."
- **low** Two quick presses: "the star goes back to what it was" doesn't say what "was" means when the first save fails after the second was sent (EXPERIENCE.md → Favourite star → At once, then saved). *Fix:* "On failure the star shows the value of the last successful save, or the value before the first press."
- **low** Clear filters moves focus to the Read chip today (`CatalogPage.tsx`, `readChipRef`). Neither spine says whether that stays, now that Favourites comes first (EXPERIENCE.md → Filter chips). *Fix:* say "focus goes to Favourites" or "focus stays on Read".
- **low** On Tiles and List the line shows under the filter row, which can be a screen or more above the star in a 500-Book grid. It is announced, but out of sight (EXPERIENCE.md → State Patterns → Couldn't save). *Fix:* accept and say "the page doesn't scroll to it", or show it near the toolbar's sticky edge.

## 7. Inheritance discipline — thin

Checked: `sources` lists the plan. "Book", "Favourite", "starred" and "Favourites" are used the same way in both files and match the plan. Component names agree across both files. The plan's mistaken "Tiles and List show covers" is not repeated: List is a table with the star in the title cell.

### Findings
- **high** Architecture AD-7's rule is "No optimistic updates", and it binds the Couldn't-save pattern. The star is optimistic (D7), and the chip-on rule hides a Book "at once", before the refetch, which needs the client to override the cached view. EXPERIENCE.md records the exception against its own State Patterns and cites AD-7 only for the refetch. Plan step 4 updates AD-18, AD-13, AD-3, AD-6 and AD-21, not AD-7. A Story 16.1 review will find code that breaks AD-7, and AGENTS.md treats that as a review finding to fix, not spine drift (EXPERIENCE.md → Favourite star → At once, then saved; plan section 3, step 4). *Fix:* say in the subsection that this needs an AD-7 exception for the star, and add AD-7 to plan step 4: "the favourite star alone may show its new value before the response, held in component state, never by patching the cached view; it is dropped on the refetch or the failure."
- **medium** "The e2e tests find it by that name", but "Favourite" is a prefix of "Favourites" (the chip) and of "Favourite *Title*". Playwright's default name match is a case-insensitive substring, so `getByRole('button', { name: 'Favourite' })` on Catalog with a popover open matches the chip too and fails strict mode. Two twins' List rows also both read "Favourite Dune" (EXPERIENCE.md → Favourite star → Control). *Fix:* "Tests match these names exactly (`exact: true`), as Form Names → Lookup rule does." Accept the twin duplicate and say so, or name the star after the tile link's full name.
- **low** After "favourite plan D1, D2" the subsection cites bare "D3", "D4", "D5", "D6", "D7", "D11", while the IA rows next to them cite the prd-deltas "D14", "D31" (EXPERIENCE.md → Favourite star). *Fix:* "favourite plan D4" on first use in each bullet, or one line in the intro: "D-numbers here are the favourite plan's."
- **low** The Brand line says gold is never "a fill", but the star itself is filled with gold. "Fills" in the Don't column has the same problem (DESIGN.md → Brand & Style; Do's and Don'ts). *Fix:* "never text, a border, a background or a message".
- **low** The existing popover test `spine-popover.spec.ts` AC-2 presses Shift+Tab from the control focused on open and expects the popover to close. With the star first in the DOM, that press now reaches the star. The spine is right; the story needs to know (EXPERIENCE.md → Spine popover → Favourite star). *Fix:* add "(this changes the existing Shift+Tab test: the star is the new first control)" to the bullet, or note it in the story. Also update the Focus bullet's "first actionable control" to "the primary action, else the first link, never the star".
