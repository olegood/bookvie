# PRD Quality Review — bookvie

Reviewed: `prd.md` and `addendum.md` (2026-10-28), judged at hobby, single-user rigor.

## Overall verdict
This is a good hobby PRD. It has a real thesis (the "urgency chain"), a Vision that could only belong to this product, NFRs with actual bounds ("under 30 seconds", "at most 2 interactions") and an unusually honest Won't list. What's at risk is the pace and interruption logic at the centre of the product. The MoSCoW split leaves the Must build without the place where paused books go. FR-16 is a Must, but every scope it applies to is deferred to Later. And "measured pace" is defined in a way that the product's own core scenario (long pauses) will distort. Each of these is a fix of a paragraph or less, but they should be fixed before stories are cut.

## Decision-readiness — adequate
Decisions are stated as decisions, not "considerations". Examples: "There are no streaks, nothing resets, and there is no abandoned status" (§1), "bookvie does not track how many pages were read on which day" (§4.3), "This is the only import path" (FR-23). §6.3 Won't rules things out explicitly, including "a local API for entry" and "per-day page logs". "Open Questions: None open" is believable for a solo tool where the builder is the user, and `.memlog.md` shows the assumptions really were put to Oleg and confirmed.

The weakness is that a few trade-offs are stated as rules without saying what they cost. Some MoSCoW placements also contradict the scope they depend on, so the PRD looks more settled than it is.

### Findings
- **[high]** FR-16 is a Must with no scope that exists in the MVP (§4.6 FR-16, §6.1, §6.2). FR-16 applies "for any scope that has a deadline (Weekend sprint, deadline goal)". §6.1 lists "Quick pace calculator (FR-15–16)" as Must, but both Weekend sprints and deadline goals sit in §6.2 Later. UJ-5's "48 pages/day needed to finish by Monday" can't happen in the MVP either. FR-15's "Sequence" scope has the same problem. *Fix:* Either move FR-16 and FR-15's Sequence scope to Later along with Sequences and Goals, or give FR-16 a scope that exists in the MVP (e.g., an ad-hoc "finish by date" field on the Book and library calculator). Then mark UJ-5's sprint sentence as Later.
- **[medium]** The cost of "all pages count in the period where it finished" is not named (§4.7 FR-17). The rule is a clear decision, but the PRD doesn't say what it gives up. A 900-page book read over November and finished on 2 December puts 900 pages in December and zero in November, so monthly page stats will swing between zero and large spikes. UJ-4's "counts every page of the book toward this month" presents this as a feature. *Fix:* Add one sentence that accepts the lumpiness on purpose (it follows from having no per-day logs), so nobody "fixes" it later by adding page logs back in.

## Substance over theater — strong
There is very little furniture. The Vision passes the swap test, because "urgency chain", "exactly one tracked main book" and "removing a state also removes a failure signal" could not move to another reading app unchanged. Each of the three pillars visibly drives decisions: Simplicity leads to no abandoned status, and Tone leads to "no red failure states" and FR-12's "Days with no type are allowed and are not shown as failures". There are no personas, which is right for a tool with one user. The NFRs are specific to this product: a 30-second catch-up, no network, desktop only. The addendum's competitive digest is short, has sources, and actually feeds the "Gaps bookvie can own" list instead of being padding.

### Findings
- **[low]** One adjective NFR (§5 Local-first). "Persistence is kept simple" is a value judgement, not a bound. The addendum gives the real intent ("no complex persistence layer… export/restore should probably share its format"). *Fix:* Restate it as a constraint, e.g., "no database server; all data lives in files the export can reproduce", or point to the addendum note.

## Strategic coherence — adequate
The thesis is explicit and good: interruptions cause context loss, which leads to abandonment, so the fix is one Main book, deliberate switches and visible paused books. The Day-type and no-streak design follows from the user-complaint evidence in the addendum. The counter-metric SM-C1 is a good one and is exactly right for this product.

The weak spots are that prioritization and metrics follow "replace the paper diary" more closely than they follow the stated thesis.

### Findings
- **[high]** The feature that delivers the thesis is a Should, while the action that creates the problem is a Must (§1, §4.2, §6.1). §1 promises "Paused books stay visible until Oleg picks them up again", and JTBD 2 is "remind me to come back to it". But Up next and Waiting for you (FR-7) are Should, while Pause (FR-6) and the switch confirmation (FR-5) are Must. In a Must-only build, Oleg can pause a book, and then nothing lists it, which recreates the "paused book is never resumed" failure the product exists to prevent. *Fix:* Promote at least the Waiting for you list (FR-7's third and fourth consequences) to Must, or add a Must consequence to FR-6 saying paused Readings are listed on the main screen.
- **[high]** No Success Metric tests the thesis (§7). SM-1 checks that the paper diary is retired and SM-2 checks that the catalog is complete. Neither checks whether interrupted books actually get resumed, which is the bet in §1. *Fix:* Add something like "SM-3: every Reading paused by an urgent book during the first 3 months is either resumed or still visible in Waiting for you. No paused Reading disappears." Waiting for you has no cap, so something like "oldest paused Reading under N months" also works.
- **[medium]** Two of the three pillars have no Must feature (§1, §6.1). "Conscious reading: a purpose at the start… 'fulfilled?' at the finish" is delivered by FR-9 Purpose and the FR-14 prompts, and both are Should. That may be a fine sequencing choice, but it isn't stated. *Fix:* Add one line to §6.1 saying the Must build delivers "Visible progress" and "Simplicity" and that "Conscious reading" arrives with the Shoulds, so the gap is deliberate.

## Done-ness clarity — adequate
Most FRs have testable consequences, and some are excellent. Examples: FR-1 "Saving is refused while any required field is empty", FR-10 "at most 2 interactions after opening the app", FR-15 "Zero, negative or non-numeric input shows no result and no error noise", FR-23 "A file exported and then restored reproduces identical data". The gaps are in the computational FRs, where an engineer would have to invent the rule.

### Findings
- **[high]** Pauses will distort "measured pace", and the formula is underspecified (§4.7 FR-17, used by FR-11 and FR-15). "pages of finished Readings ÷ days from start to finish" counts paused time as reading time. The product's core scenario (UJ-3: a book paused for weeks by an urgent work book) will make the pace for exactly those books far too slow. That error then flows into the "~7 evenings left" effort figure and the pre-filled calculator value. The PRD also doesn't say how several Readings are combined (mean of ratios? total pages ÷ total days? the last N only?), and doesn't cover the cold start: before the first finish there is no pace, so UJ-2's "~7 evenings left" can't appear. *Fix:* Store the pause and resume dates for each Reading. These are state changes, not per-day page logs, so they stay consistent with §6.3. Define pace as total pages of finished Readings ÷ total active (unpaused) days. State the cold-start behaviour, e.g., "the effort figure is hidden and the calculator field is empty until one Reading is finished".
- **[medium]** The library scope double-counts pages and contradicts itself on re-reads (§4.6 FR-15). "the unread pages of owned Books, plus the pages left in the active and paused Readings" counts a Book paused at p.120 of 300 twice: 300 because the Book is unread, plus 180 left in the Reading. "finished Books are excluded" also conflicts with "pages left in the active… Readings" when the active Reading is a re-read of a finished Book. *Fix:* "Library = all pages of owned Books with no Reading, plus the pages left in every active or paused Reading, including re-reads."
- **[medium]** There are no bounds on the page update (§4.3 FR-10). The PRD doesn't say what happens with a page above the Book's page count, page 0, or a page lower than the current one (going back to re-read). An accidental last-page entry triggers the FR-14 finish flow, and nothing describes an undo. *Fix:* Add consequences: page is clamped or rejected outside 1..pages, lowering the page is allowed, and the finish flow can be cancelled, or a finished Reading can be reopened.
- **[medium]** The duplicate match rule is not defined (§4.1 FR-3). The PRD doesn't say whether "a match on title and author" is exact or case- and whitespace-insensitive, or whether it means any author or all authors when there are several. It also doesn't say whether ISBN-10 and ISBN-13 count as the same number or whether hyphens are ignored. With AI-driven bulk entry (UJ-1), this decides whether the warning fires too often or never. *Fix:* "Case- and whitespace-insensitive title match plus at least one shared author. ISBNs are compared with hyphens removed."
- **[low]** The sort options are not specified (§4.9 FR-20). "and sort it" doesn't say by which fields. *Fix:* List the sort keys (e.g., title, author, date added, pages).
- **[low]** The Book type list is open-ended (§4.1 FR-1). "type (paperback, hardcover, ebook, …)" leaves the list unclosed, but `.memlog.md` says type drives donation eligibility, so the list needs to be fixed or extensible on purpose. *Fix:* List the values, or say "user-extensible; ebook is the only non-physical type".

## Scope honesty — adequate
This is the PRD's strongest discipline. §6.3 Won't is explicit and specific ("an abandoned status, or any history of abandons or exclusions", "a session timer"). §6.2 Later is detailed enough that deferred ideas aren't lost. FR-2's "Out of Scope: API endpoints for entry" removes a likely wrong assumption. Open-item density is close to zero, which is fine at these stakes. The problems are the places where the MoSCoW split itself creates a silent gap (see the FR-16 and FR-7 findings above) and one capability nobody addressed.

### Findings
- **[medium]** Deleting a Book or a Reading is never addressed (§4.1 FR-1, §5 Data safety). FR-1 covers "create and edit" only. UJ-1's AI bulk entry and FR-3's "add anyway" will both produce mistaken Books, and the PRD doesn't say whether deleting is a Won't, a confirmed action, or impossible. §5 "no action silently deletes reading history" suggests deletion exists but doesn't say so. *Fix:* Add an FR-1 consequence: "A Book with no Readings can be deleted after confirmation. A Book with Readings cannot be deleted in v1." Or list deletion under Won't.
- **[low]** Ownership has only one possible value in the MVP (§3, FR-1, FR-4, FR-20). Donation is Later, so every Book is "owned", yet FR-4 "Ownership is shown on each Book" and FR-20 filtering "by ownership" are in scope. The cost is small, but it should be a deliberate choice. *Fix:* Mark both as no-ops until Donation ships, or move them to Later.

## Downstream usability — adequate
The PRD is chain-top (§0 names UX, architecture and epics as consumers), so this dimension matters. The Glossary is present and is mostly applied consistently: capitalized Book, Reading, Main book and Day type are used carefully. FR IDs are contiguous (FR-1 to FR-23), UJs have the named protagonist Oleg, and each Must/Should line in §6.1 points back to FR numbers. The addendum's "Treat renaming those labels as a breaking change" is useful guidance for architecture.

### Findings
- **[medium]** Up next is defined as a Book but moves into a list of Readings (§3, FR-7, UJ-3). Glossary: Up next is "the single Book Oleg has chosen to read next", and Waiting for you is "the list of paused Readings". FR-7 says a displaced Up next "moves to Waiting for you". If that Up next was a never-started Book, it has no Reading, so it can't join a list of paused Readings. Data modelling and stories will split on this. *Fix:* Say that a displaced, never-started Up next simply returns to the catalog, and only a displaced Up next that has a paused Reading moves to Waiting for you.
- **[medium]** The label contract is asserted but never written down (§4.1 FR-2, addendum). "a stable accessible name that does not change between releases without notice" is only testable if the names exist somewhere. The addendum calls them "the contract", but no list of them exists. *Fix:* Add a short field-and-button label table to the addendum (Title, Pages, Author, … "Add book", "Search titles"), so UX and tests have one source.
- **[low]** "Realizes" links are missing for some features (§4.5, §4.7–4.10). Sections 4.1–4.4 and 4.6 cite their UJ, but §4.5 (Finish) doesn't cite UJ-4, which it clearly realizes. *Fix:* Add "Realizes UJ-4." to §4.5.

## Shape fit — strong
The shape is right for a solo tool that feeds downstream workflows: five short UJs that each map to a feature, a capability-spec FR list, a glossary, and no personas, market sizing or enterprise sections. The UJs pull their weight because they carry concrete numbers ("p.212… 64%… ~7 evenings") that turn into acceptance examples.

### Findings
- **[low]** The PRD is longer than the agreed size (whole document). `.memlog.md` records "~2-page PRD, overflow to addendum", and the PRD runs to about 290 lines (roughly 5 pages). Most of the extra length is earned. The detailed §6.2 Later list could move to the addendum without loss. *Fix:* Optional. Move §6.2's sub-bullets (Goals, Sequences, Donation detail) to the addendum and keep the one-line headings.

## Mechanical notes
- **ID continuity:** FR-1 to FR-23 have no gaps or duplicates. UJ-1 to UJ-5 are contiguous. SM-1, SM-2 and SM-C1 are fine. All cross-references (FR-5 from FR-7, FR-14 from FR-10, FR-17 from FR-11 and FR-15, FR-19 from FR-12) resolve.
- **Assumptions Index roundtrip:** There are no inline `[ASSUMPTION]` tags, and §9 says all were confirmed on 2026-10-28. This matches `.memlog.md` line 48 ("All 6 draft assumptions confirmed"). Consistent.
- **Glossary drift and forward references:** §3 Pause says "Marathons can be paused too", and §3 defines Donation and Sequence, but all three are Later. It's harmless, but it's worth tagging them "(Later)" in the Glossary so UX doesn't design for them. FR-11's "evenings" is not a glossary unit and is computed from a per-calendar-day pace. Either rename it to "days" or define "evening = one day at measured pace".
- **Tautology:** FR-17's "only Main book Readings count" says nothing, because Side reads have no Readings (§3, FR-8). Either drop it or reword it as "Side reads never count".
- **Split FR:** FR-14 carries both a Must part (the finish date) and a Should part (the prompts), and §6.1 relies on "FR-14 core" and "FR-14 prompts". Consider splitting it into FR-14a and FR-14b so the stories split cleanly.
- **Example consistency:** UJ-4 says "toward this month", but FR-17 supports any period. These don't conflict, but "toward the period it finished in" matches the rule.
- **Required sections:** Everything appropriate for hobby stakes is present. Nothing is missing.
