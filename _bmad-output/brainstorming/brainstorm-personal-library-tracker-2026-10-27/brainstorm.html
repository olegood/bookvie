Book I · first batch

Reverse Brainstorming

How could a reading app make you fail? Fourteen failures were dreamt up, then every one was flipped. Each failure is a flash card: tap it to turn it over and see its flip.

The trap (coach insight). A strict WIP limit combined with shameful abandoning freezes reading entirely. For a WIP limit to work, abandoning has to be cheap and dignified.

Marginalia from the flips

Each flip started a conversation. Open a thread to read it in order.
