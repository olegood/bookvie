# Epic 14 Context: See card notes as boxes

<!-- Generated from planning artifacts. Regenerate with compile-epic-context if planning docs change. -->

## Goal

The unscoped Card notes page gains a Boxes view: one box per Book that has notes, drawn in the Book's spine color with a pile of cards that grows with its note count. A remembered Boxes · List toggle keeps today's page as List. Opening a box opens that Book's scoped page under a strip in its spine color. A search on the shelf fades the boxes without a match and shows "3 of 12" on those with matches, and the search text goes into the box and comes back out with the user. Oleg can then sit with one Book's ideas or hunt across all his cards without retyping. The page keeps its name "Card notes", its `h1` and its `/card-notes` route, and "Card note" stays the domain and agent-tool term.

## Stories

- Story 14.1: Carry each Book's spine color in the card-notes view
- Story 14.2: See my card notes as boxes
- Story 14.3: See the open box in its Book's color
- Story 14.4: Search the boxes
- Story 14.5: Keep the search when I open a box and come back

## Requirements & Constraints

- **List is unchanged.** List view is the unscoped page as it was before Epic 14: groups, search with highlights and "N notes match.", Edit, "Go through as a pile", Back to top. The existing Card notes e2e specs pass with no edit to their files, except the Story 5.3 chip leaf in `card-notes.spec.ts` that Story 14.5's AC-3 rewrote (removing the chip now carries the search): one option fixture in `frontend/e2e/fixtures.ts` stores `bookvie.cardNotesView = "list"` before each test, and new Epic 14 specs turn it off.
- **Boxes is the default** when nothing is stored, the value is empty or junk, or the storage read throws. The toggle and count line show only on the unscoped page, only with at least one card note, never on the open box, and never while loading or under "Couldn't load". With no notes at all the page keeps "No card notes yet."
- **Only Books with notes get a box,** in the view's group order. Neither view sorts.
- **The fade is never the only signal.** A faded box stays in place, stays a link and a Tab stop, keeps a full-strength focus ring, and carries its match in its accessible name, with its visible count ("*Refactoring*, 4 card notes, no match"). The fade is a documented deviation (UX-DR93). Otherwise WCAG 2.2 AA in both themes and under `forced-colors: active`.
- **Motion:** lift and fade animate (150ms ease-out) only while the field's text changes. A carried search, or a List → Boxes switch with text in the field, renders the search state at once. Under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`, changes are instant.
- **UX-DR92 staging** (Boxes with text showed the List's results) existed only from 14.2 to 14.4. 14.4 removed it from the code and from EXPERIENCE.md; it must not come back.
- **Gate:** the per-epic quality gates, plus the P0 risks green: List unchanged; default and storage (including the key never in an export); `spineColor` on every group and on the scope; the shelf search; the carried search both ways (Back and the chip), with every other way in starting empty.
- **Out of scope:** boxes the user packs across Books, renaming the page, a pile button on the box, and anything only Safari or Firefox shows. No story needs a Mac check.

## Technical Decisions

- **Server (14.1 only):** `CardNotesGroup` and `CardNotesScope` carry `spineColor`, the Book's stored resolved `spine_color` exactly as stored (the same value the `book` and `catalog` views give), on the scope even when the Book has no notes. No other server, data or backup-format change. `schema.d.ts` is regenerated by `api:gen`, never hand-formatted. `get_card_notes` returns the view as is with an unchanged description.
- **One place for card-note rules:** `lib/cardNotes.ts` holds the single matcher (contains, ignoring case; spaces-only counts as empty) used by the List filter, the open box, each box's "M of N" and the shelf sentence, so views and a carried search can't count differently. It also computes the pile step (1 → 1 card, 2–5 → 3, 6–20 → 5, 21+ → 7), the count line's totals (summed over loaded groups), each box's accessible name and the match counts. The page computes none of these. One box is `components/CardNoteBox`; the toggle reuses the generic `components/ViewToggle`.
- **Color:** a box front and the open box strip use `spineColor` exactly, read or unread, never the `spine-unread-*` colors.
- **Remembered view:** `bookvie.cardNotesView` (`"boxes"` / `"list"`) via `lib/cardNotesView.ts`, like `lib/catalogView.ts`, inside try/catch. Stored as soon as it changes; a failed write keeps the choice for the visit. Never exported; a restore neither brings nor replaces it.
- **Carried search (14.5), which supersedes the plan's "router state for the text":**
  - The text lives only in React state of the route component `CardNotesPage`: never in the URL, storage or `history.state`.
  - Router state, built by the page, carries only navigation facts: a carry marker and, on a box link's push, `openedFrom` (the shelf entry's `location.key`, `"default"` compared like any other). The chip's push carries the marker. The marker is ignored on mount.
  - The text is kept only on a PUSH carrying the marker, or on a POP that lands on the `openedFrom` of the entry just left. Every other `location.key` change starts empty (nav link REPLACE, other links, Forward, other POPs; reloads and arrivals from other routes remount). While the view has no groups the text is dropped. Switching Boxes ⇄ List is no navigation and keeps the text.
  - The decision runs during render, never in an effect and never by writing a ref in render. The page keeps the last handled `{ key, state }` in state and updates it with the text in the same render (`HomePage`'s hand-off pattern). A unit test runs it under `<StrictMode>`.
  - The body is keyed by `location.key`: a fresh view query with no placeholder data, and fresh pile, panels, pending count and focus maps. Each key change scrolls to the top (`behavior: 'auto'`), then focuses the `h1`. A carried search is announced once, from the new entry's view once it is settled (`ready` and not fetching) and the `h1` has focus, by an effect that cancels on cleanup.
- **Styling:** every value comes from DESIGN.md's components block (`card-note-box`, `card-notes-view-toggle`, `card-notes-count-line`, `open-box-strip`). Any literal in `frontend/src/styles/tokens.css` matches DESIGN.md character for character (`e2e/app-shell.spec.ts`), and that file is never formatted. The box's label, cards, count and pill are fixed literals; in dark theme only the page and the shadow's strength change.

## UX & Interaction Patterns

- **Toolbar:** Search card notes, then the "Card notes view" toggle (`role="group"`, Boxes then List, `aria-pressed`, text only) and the count line, `spacing.4` apart. A press switches in place, keeps focus on the pressed button and the search text, and leaves `status` silent.
- **Shelf grid:** left-aligned `repeat(auto-fill, 216px)`, `spacing.6` column and `spacing.8` row gaps, every box 216 × 196. A search never hides or reflows a box.
- **Box:** a link to `/card-notes?book=<id>`, one Tab stop, named "*Title*, N card notes" with the full title even when the 2-line label truncates it. Focus is a `2px solid accent` ring at 4px offset, `rounded.lg`. No hover change besides the pointer.
- **Count line:** "139 card notes in 9 Books" in `meta`/`ink-tertiary`, plain text, Boxes view only. During a search it becomes "N card notes in M Books contain “habit”." or "No card notes contain “…”." (quoting the trimmed text), which `status` announces after about 500 ms without typing. Esc or emptying the field restores every box and the totals.
- **Search state on a box:** matches lift 6px and show an "M of N" pill in place of the count; non-matches fade to 0.38 through one inner wrapper (cards and front) so the focus ring stays full strength.
- **Open box:** the scoped page as before Epic 14 (chip, search, Add card note, notes, pile, Back to top), no toggle, plus an 8px full-width `aria-hidden` strip in `scope.spineColor` directly under the app shell, shown only once the view has loaded, also for a Book with no notes.
- **Carried search arrival:** on an open box, only matching notes with `<mark>`, `h1` focused, page at top, then `status` says "3 notes match." / "1 note matches." / "0 notes match." once (a faded box's open box shows "No card notes contain “…”." and keeps the pile). Back or removing the chip returns to the shelf in the remembered view with the open box's current text, announcing the view's own count once. The URL never holds the text.
- Back to top keeps working in both views and on the open box, moving focus to Search card notes.
