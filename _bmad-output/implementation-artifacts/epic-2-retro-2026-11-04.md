| ID | Finding | Source | Disposition |
|---|---|---|---|
| F-R1 | Archive cover names are compared with `casefold()` but not Unicode-normalized. On macOS (APFS ignores normalization), `covers/é.jpg` in NFC form and in NFD form pass the duplicate check and unpack into the same staged file, so one Book silently gets the other's cover. `replace_all`'s `os.path.lexists` has the same blind spot against existing `covers/`. Reproduced by the reviewer. Only a hand-crafted archive reaches it, because minted names are lowercase UUIDs. | `backend/bookvie/store/restore.py:217-220`, `:233-240`; `store/store.py` `replace_all` | **fix now** (action 2). | F-R2 | An `OSError` that the archive's own entry name causes (for example ENAMETOOLONG for `covers/<300 chars>.jpg`) is reported as 503 `couldnt_save` ("Couldn't save") instead of a 422 refusal. The user is told their disk failed. Reproduced by the reviewer. | `store/restore.py:113-116` (`_copy_entry` open), `intents/restore.py:109-111` | **fix now** (action 2). |

The reviewer found these clean (details in its report, summarized here):
- The export zip layout matches what restore accepts, with or without the `covers/` directory entry, and the round trip is byte-identical.
- The export builds its zip under the lock and sends it afterwards.
- `local-state.json` is never exported and restore refuses it.
- The `X-Bookvie-Export` guard requires exactly one header with the value `1`.
- The size limits count bytes actually written, including entries whose headers understate their size, and the CRC is checked.
- `.staging/` is emptied before and after every restore.
- `replace_all` and `apply` share `_commit_and_swap`, and a failure undoes only the covers that restore wrote.
- The upload body cap works.
- The Backup page's busy, focus and live-region handling holds.
