### Diff-scope review (cross-story boundary)

- **E16-R1, a stuck pending unstar steals focus and swallows keystrokes (medium; fix now → item 107).**
  - **Source:** `frontend/src/pages/CatalogPage.tsx:267-288` (the layout effect depends on `shown`, and `pendingUnstar` is cleared only when the Book leaves `shown` or the chip goes off) and `api/intents.ts:49` (`sendIntent` returns `ok` after a failed refetch).
  - **Scenario:** Favourites is on. An unstar commits, and the catalog refetch after it fails once. The Book stays shown and `pendingUnstar` stays set. Any later change that drops the Book from `shown` then fires the effect: a Search keystroke, another chip, the next star's refetch. The effect moves focus to a neighbour and speaks a stale line.
  - **Probe** (boundary reviewer, Chromium, scratchpad `probe/stale.spec.ts`):
    - Typing "Bl" in Search titles leaves "B" in the box. Focus jumps to the Bleak House tile after the first key.
    - Unstarring a second Book overwrites the pending one, and Emma's unstar is never announced.
  - **Why now:**
    - The 16.2 build triage (Edge 1, Blind 1–2) and the code review (Rejected #1) both rejected it as "needs the local server to fail in milliseconds". That rarity argument covers how the state starts.
    - The probe shows that once the state starts, it lasts until a later refetch drops the Book.
    - The automate step (`eae3f84`, `favourites-chip.spec.ts:791`) reaches it on purpose and pins half of it as intended behavior: it stays silent while waiting, and the chip-off line replaces the count (`automation-summary.md:69`, "If the retro reopens it, this leaf is the one to change").
    - The chip-on half, which steals focus, is neither handled nor tested.
  - **Prevention:** a "rejected as rare" with a known trigger needs to say what happens after the trigger. Here the automate step found the trigger was one 503. Recorded as a lesson in item 107's spec; no separate process file.
- **E16-R2, a lost response after a committed unstar with the chip on (low; accept).**
  - **Source:** `api/intents.ts:44-49` with `useFavourite.ts:99-100`.
  - **Scenario:** the server commits the unstar and the connection drops before the response arrives. The flow reports `couldntSave`, but the refetch hides the tile, so focus falls to `body` and "Your data wasn't changed." is wrong.
  - **Why accept:** "couldn't save after commit" predates the epic, applies to every intent, and needs a dropped local connection.
